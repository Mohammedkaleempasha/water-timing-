About WaterWatch

Empowering Communities Through Smart Water Visibility

WaterWatch was conceived as a human-centered Student Ideathon prototype addressing the unpredictable water supply timing challenges faced by millions of urban households in Hyderabad, Telangana.

The Problem

In rapidly expanding metropolises like Hyderabad, municipal water is distributed on alternate days or erratic timing shifts. Residents frequently face:

  • Zero real-time visibility: Water releases can vary from early dawn to midnight without prior alerts.
  • Domestic disruption: Family members wake up multiple times during the night or miss shifts to switch on booster pumps.
  • Water wastage & empty tanks: Overflowing sumps or missed fills lead to expensive private water tanker dependencies.

The WaterWatch Solution

WaterWatch synthesizes predictable scheduling with crowdsourced community verification to create an actionable neighbourhood radar:

  • Timely Advance Alerts: Know 30 to 60 minutes beforehand when valve operators open lines.
  • Crowd Confirmation Consensus: Real neighbours verify with one tap when water actually reaches taps.
  • Reliability Metrics: Transparent punctuality logs help citizens and resident welfare associations (RWAs) plan effectively.
Design Thinking Personas

Designed For Everyday Hyderabad Citizens

Local Households

Homemakers and families managing domestic water storage, laundry, and cooking without endless tap monitoring.

Working Professionals

IT and corporate employees in Hitec City, Madhapur, and Gachibowli who need predictable morning shower and storage timings.

Students & Hostels

Students living in shared flats or PGs in Ameerpet and Kukatpally who need advance alerts before leaving for college or exams.

Elderly Residents

Senior citizens spared from physical strain and sleepless nights checking outdoor sumps or heavy overhead tank valves.
